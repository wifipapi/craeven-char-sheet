Dragonlord combat doctrine

The flyby is the default — not the only way a Dragonlord fights.
Mounted striker: Miasmaeys approaches → Craeven attacks → Miasmaeys Disengages → retreat and reset.
Change modes: Stay and tank when the party needs an anchor; go all-in when the target must die now; translate the same movement doctrine underwater.
Escape: Miasmaeys' movement is part of Craeven's defense. Do not spend it casually.

Concentration warning: Hunter's Mark, Hex, Spirit Shroud, Haste, Shield of Faith, most smite spells, and several defensive auras compete with one another. Pick the combat plan first; do not try to stack concentration effects that cannot coexist.

Example: all-in single-target hit

  1. Enter with Maul form; have a soul stored if possible.
  2. Use a bonus-action smite spell before attacking if the concentration slot is worth it.
  3. Turn on Great Weapon Master when your chance to hit supports it.
  4. Land Dream Executioner: weapon dice + flat modifier + GWM + Improved Divine Smite 1d8.
  5. After the hit is confirmed, press the highest Divine Smite button you are willing to spend.
  6. If it was a natural 20, the weapon and applicable damage dice are doubled; this is your premium burst window.
  7. If the lantern is full and the target's area is worthwhile, trigger Soul Explosion.

Example: defensive hold

  1. Precast Aid or Death Ward when you have warning.
  2. Choose one concentration plan: Shield of Faith for AC, Compelled Duel for one target, or a defensive aura for the encounter's damage/control profile.
  3. Keep allies inside your 10-ft Aura of Protection whenever enemy saves are the real threat.
  4. Use Dragon's Wrath against groups or Scorn the Unworthy when prone/concentration disruption changes the fight.
  5. Let Miasmaeys attack instead of Disengaging when you deliberately choose to hold the line.
  6. Use Lay on Hands before attrition forces you out of the position you are protecting.

Smite discipline

  1. Critical hit: strongest default reason to spend a high slot; the smite dice double.
  2. Priority target must die now: spend enough to cross the expected remaining HP, not simply the highest slot.
  3. Undead / fiend: the extra d8 improves slot efficiency.
  4. Routine target: keep slots. Your weapon, Improved Divine Smite and mobility already do work.
  5. Do not pre-commit: Smite is chosen after the hit. Preserve the option until the roll matters.

Great Weapon Master

  1. Turn it on most confidently when attacking with advantage.
  2. Use it against targets whose AC is low enough that -5 does not erase the value of +10 damage.
  3. Turn it off when the only important thing is landing the hit — especially when you intend to Smite.
  4. A critical hit or reducing a creature to 0 can open the bonus-action attack from the feat.

          Craeven

          Power without loyalty is only noise.

          Eighteen. Noble. Blue half-dragon. Paladin of the Dragonlord oath. He believes the blood of dragons marks him as exceptional, but he measures superiority by what a person can become — not by how easily they can be humiliated.

          Emrys is the person whose magical power Craeven respects most. Miasmaeys is not simply his dragon; each regards the other as the part of himself that had always been missing.

          Personality

          • If someone injures him, he intends to answer decisively.
          • He faces problems head-on; direct solutions are preferable to hesitation.
          • He enjoys strength and the act of becoming stronger.
          • His humor is dry, sarcastic and sometimes vicious toward enemies.

          Ideals, bonds & flaws

          • Power: if more power can be attained, he wants to know what it costs.
          • Family: blood and chosen bond run deeper than convenience.
          • Bond: he will face any challenge to win the approval and respect of his family.
          • Flaw: he believes draconic blood makes him superior, and upbringing taught him to wrestle with that belief rather than pretend it is absent.
